TraceX

Maritime Intelligence & "Rewind the Ocean" Origin Attribution

Combines high-resolution satellite imagery, global AIS vessel tracking, and physics-based ocean current modeling to reverse-engineer oil slick drift and attribute responsibility to specific marine vessels.

✨ Core Innovation: "Rewind the Ocean"

Instead of just detecting the slick where it is floating now, TraceX computes backward hydrodynamic advection over time to pinpoint where the dump occurred, correlating that spatio-temporal corridor with historic AIS vessel tracks.
